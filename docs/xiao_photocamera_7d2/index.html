Photo as Webcam Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

What the extension stores

Photo as Webcam processes the JPG, PNG or WebP photo you choose. It re-encodes the photo at a limited size, removing the original file metadata, and keeps the processed copy in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. The extension keeps that copy until you replace or delete it, or remove the extension. The extension does not upload your photo to the developer's servers or sync it between devices.

What a website can access

Choosing a photo saves a local copy. To use it on a compatible web page, open the extension on that page and explicitly enable photo mode. The extension then makes the processed image available to that page as a replacement camera feed. The enabled page can access, copy, record or transmit the image and may keep copies under its own policies. Choose an image you are comfortable sharing with that page. A full page reload or navigation to a new page requires you to enable photo mode again.

For pages where you enable the photo, the extension temporarily keeps Chrome’s tab and document identifiers in browser session storage so it can later stop the correct video output. It does not send those identifiers to a server.

Chrome permissions

  • storage keeps the processed image on this device and supports saving, previewing, replacing and deleting it.
  • activeTab grants temporary access to the current tab after you open the extension. The extension supplies photos only after you enable photo mode on that page.
  • scripting runs the extension's bundled camera script in the enabled tab's main page and controls the photo feed there.

Chrome continues to manage microphone requests and permissions.

Free usage and membership

The extension keeps the number of remaining free photo saves in Chrome's local extension storage. If you choose to view membership pricing, the extension opens ShipLang's account and payment service. It sends the product ID, Chrome extension ID, extension version, browser language, and any existing ShipLang user ID and membership expiry to that service. The extension saves membership details returned by ShipLang on this device to verify your access. The payment service does not receive your selected photo.

ShipLang provides sign-in and payment services for the independent developer of this extension. If you sign in with Google, account information such as your email address and profile image is used to manage your ShipLang account and membership. See the Google Privacy Policy and ShipLang Privacy Policy. If you purchase a membership, the checkout identifies the payment provider that processes your payment details. The extension does not receive your card details.

To request deletion of your ShipLang membership data, email support@shiplang.com and include the extension name or its download link. ShipLang states that it retains membership data until deletion is requested and handles deletion requests within seven days.

Stopping and deleting

Use the extension to stop photo mode on the current page or delete the selected photo. Stopping ends the extension's photo output on that page and retains the local copy. Deleting removes the stored copy and stops its output on enabled pages. Neither action erases copies that a website already made. After stopping photo mode, restart the camera on the page to use Chrome's normal camera behavior. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension data under Chrome's normal handling.

Optional links and contact

Photo as Webcam does not use analytics, advertising or tracking services. Clicking Test camera opens a separate website. If you enable photo mode there, the page can access the feed like any other enabled page. Use of that site is subject to the site's privacy policy. The contact link opens a mail service.

For privacy questions, email e@shiplang.com.
