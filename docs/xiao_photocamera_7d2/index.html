Photo as Webcam Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers the Photo as Webcam Chrome extension, listed as Image as Webcam in U.S. English and 照片替代摄像头 in Simplified Chinese. The extension lets you use one selected photo as the camera feed on a compatible web page that you choose.

Chrome permissions

storage
Stores the processed photo and its format, the number of free saves remaining, and any membership details returned by ShipLang in Chrome’s local extension storage. Chrome’s session storage keeps temporary tab and document IDs so the extension can stop the correct photo feed. The extension uses local and session storage on this device. It does not use Chrome Sync.
activeTab
Grants temporary access to the current tab when you open the extension. The extension checks the current page to determine whether it can provide a photo feed there. It only supplies the photo after you select “Enable photo on this page.”
scripting
Runs the bundled camera script in the top-level document of the page where you enable photo mode. The script supplies the selected photo as the video feed and stops the output when you disable it.

These are the three permissions requested in the extension manifest. Chrome manages the website’s normal camera and microphone permissions.

Photos and the pages you enable

The extension processes the JPG, PNG or WebP photo you choose. It re-encodes the image, limits its size and strips the original file metadata. The processed copy is saved on this device until you replace it, delete it or uninstall the extension. The extension does not upload the photo to the developer or ShipLang.

A web page can read, copy, record or transmit the photo feed after you enable it for that page. This includes scripts running on that page. Copies a website makes are subject to that website’s policies. Refreshing or navigating to a new page requires you to enable photo mode again. The extension does not send the tab and document IDs used for this feature to a server.

Optional login and payments

When you choose to view membership pricing, the extension opens the ShipLang account and payment service at crx.shiplang.com. The request contains the product ID, Chrome extension ID, extension version, browser interface language and, if already saved, your ShipLang user ID and membership expiry. The extension stores the membership details returned by ShipLang on this device to check access.

ShipLang provides sign-in and payment services to the independent extension developer. Google sign-in is used to establish your identity. The account and membership service uses information such as your email address, profile image and membership expiry to manage access. See the Google Privacy Policy and the ShipLang Privacy Policy.

If you purchase a membership, the service displayed at checkout processes the payment information you provide. If Stripe is offered at checkout, see the Stripe Privacy Policy. The extension does not receive your payment card details or send your photo to the payment service.

ShipLang says its sign-in and membership data is kept until deletion is requested and that it does not retain backups of that data. Email support@shiplang.com with the extension name or its download link, what you want deleted and why. ShipLang says it handles requests within seven days of receipt.

Stop sharing, delete your photo or contact us

“Stop using photo” stops the output for the current page and keeps the local image. “Delete photo” clears the saved image and stops the extension’s photo output on enabled pages. These actions do not delete copies a website has already made. Uninstalling the extension removes its local extension data under Chrome’s normal handling. To delete membership data held by ShipLang, use the request process above.

The “Test camera” link opens a separate test page. The extension handles a photo on that page in the same way as on other enabled pages. The contact link opens a mail service.

For questions about this extension and policy, contact the developer at e@shiplang.com.
