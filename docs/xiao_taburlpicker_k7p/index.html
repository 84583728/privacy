Privacy Policy for copy urls from all tabs

Effective November 28, 2026.

Tab URLs and the tabs permission

When you open the popup, the extension reads the URLs of tabs Chrome makes available across its windows. The tabs permission is required to read those URLs. The extension displays them in an editable list and uses each URL's hostname to filter the list. It can also remove exact duplicates. The extension does not read page contents or query your browsing history.

Local processing, copying and exporting

The extension processes URLs and your edits in the popup and does not send them to the developer or any third party. It does not use analytics, an account, browser sync, or server storage. When you click Copy, the extension writes the current list to your clipboard. When you click Export CSV, Chrome saves a CSV file on your device.

Retention and deletion

The extension holds the current list while the popup is open. Closing the popup discards the list and the extension's copy of your edits. You can replace clipboard content and delete downloaded files using your device's controls. The extension does not keep a copy or a backup of either output.

External links

The example and support links open separate websites when clicked. The extension does not include your list of URLs in these links. If you choose to send a support email, your email provider and the recipient will receive the material you submit.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
