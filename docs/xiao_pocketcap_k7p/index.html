Privacy policy

This policy describes data processing for HttpPacket (product ID: xiao_pocketcap_k7p). Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Web request data

The extension captures network activity in the browser tab you choose, including URLs, request and response headers, bodies, timing, connection details, and WebSocket messages where the browser makes them available. Captures may include cookies, authorization values, personal information, or sensitive business data contained in the page traffic. These records are processed on your device for viewing, editing, replaying, breakpoints, rewriting, local mapping, search, history, and export. Captured traffic is not uploaded to a developer capture server.

Local storage and retention

Records, bodies, favorites, and history are stored in the extension’s local IndexedDB. Preferences, rules, diagnostics, trial counters, account information, and membership status are stored in Chrome local storage. They are retained until you delete the relevant entries, clear extension data, or uninstall the extension. Exports you save remain wherever you save them and must be deleted separately. The extension does not use Chrome Sync for these records. Display language preferences are stored locally. Live captures are limited to the latest 500 requests and up to 256 KiB per body; saved history retention follows your settings. Local data is stored using browser storage and is not separately encrypted by the extension.

Requests you initiate

Replaying or sending a request contacts the destination you choose and can send the headers, body, and browser-managed credentials relevant to that request. DNS tools contact Google DNS or Cloudflare DNS with the domain and query type you enter. Website icons are retrieved from the applicable website. These services and destination websites receive the network information normally required to serve those requests.

Accounts, memberships, and payments

If you choose to sign in or open payment, the extension opens ShipLang-hosted account and payment services. The flow supplies the product ID, extension ID, extension version, interface language, and any previously stored user ID and membership expiry. Account and membership results are stored locally. ShipLang stores the email address, avatar, and membership expiry needed for these services and protects this information. The developer cooperates with ShipLang, which provides AI development software and sign-in/payment infrastructure; this extension is the developer’s product and does not represent ShipLang. No captured request bodies or headers are sent by the account bridge. After an account identifier is available, the extension registers an uninstall feedback URL with the account identifier and extension version; Chrome may open this service page when the extension is uninstalled.

Service retention and deletion

ShipLang retains account and membership data until you request deletion, with no backups retained after deletion. Email support@shiplang.com with the extension name or download link and your deletion request and reason. Requests are handled within seven days. You may also contact the developer at e@shiplang.com. Local capture data is deleted using extension controls, by clearing extension data, or by uninstalling; it is not held on the account service.

Permissions

The debugger permission connects to your selected tab to capture and debug requests and perform interception or replay. The storage permission saves preferences, rules, trial and membership state locally. Host access to dns.google and cloudflare-dns.com is used for DNS tools you explicitly run. The extension does not request a persistent all-sites content script permission.

Third-party privacy information

Google sign-in is subject to Google’s privacy policy. DNS requests are subject to the privacy practices of the selected DNS provider. Payment is handled by the checkout provider displayed on the hosted checkout page; consult its policy before payment. If that provider is PayPro Global or Stripe, their policies are linked below. The extension does not collect payment-card details in its own pages.

Policy changes and contact

Material changes to data processing will be reflected in an updated policy. Contact the developer at e@shiplang.com for questions about the extension. For service-account deletion, contact support@shiplang.com.
