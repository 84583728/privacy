Privacy Policy

AI Image Generator Free No Sign Up · Last updated: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what the browser extension “AI Image Generator Free No Sign Up” (the “extension”) stores and sends when you use it. The extension turns a text description into a picture. It has no account of its own, no analytics, and no advertising.

What the extension handles

  • The description you type, together with the style and aspect ratio you selected, is sent to our image generation provider so a picture can be created. This happens only when you press the generate button.
  • The generated image is delivered by the same provider and shown in the extension. When you press download, the image file is saved by your browser to your own computer.
  • Your settings and local state — the last description, selected style, aspect ratio, the link of the last generated image, and how many free generations are left — are stored in your browser’s local extension storage.
  • Membership status, if you choose to sign in, is stored in the same local storage so the extension knows whether unlimited generation is active. It contains the account identifier, the product identifier and the membership expiry time.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Image generation provider. Your description text, the selected style and the requested image size are sent to pollinations.ai, which creates and hosts the image. No account identifier, e-mail address or browsing history is included in that request.
  • Sign-in and payment. Sign-in and payment open a separate window hosted by ShipLang at crx.shiplang.com. ShipLang stores the e-mail address, profile picture and membership expiry needed to run the membership. If you sign in with Google, the Google sign-in flow is governed by the Google privacy policy. Card details are entered on the payment page and are never received by the extension or by the developer.
  • Nothing else. No browsing history, no page content, no keystrokes and no other tabs are read or transmitted. History, clipboard and website access permissions are not requested.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — remembers your description, style, aspect ratio, remaining free generations and membership status on your own device.
  • https://image.pollinations.ai/* — allows the extension to request the generated image and to save it as a file. No other website is contacted.

Where data is kept and for how long

  • Descriptions, settings and counters stay in your browser’s local extension storage until you clear them, remove the extension, or overwrite them by generating again.
  • Images are hosted by the generation provider and downloaded to your computer; the extension keeps only the link of your most recent image.
  • Membership data held by ShipLang is kept until you ask for deletion. No backup copy is kept after deletion. To request deletion, e-mail support@shiplang.com, name the extension or its listing link, and say what you want removed and why; requests are handled within 7 days.
  • The developer sells no data, shares no data with advertisers, and runs no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Your choices

  • You can use the extension without signing in; the free generations do not require an account.
  • Remove the extension at any time to delete its local storage. Uninstalling does not affect a membership you already paid for; sign in again after reinstalling to restore it.
  • To stop descriptions from leaving your browser, simply do not press the generate button.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the extension starts handling new data, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact
