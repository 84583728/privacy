Privacy Policy

Amazon Review Exporter - CSV & Bulk Reviews

Last updated: 2026-11-06

This policy explains how this independent Chrome extension handles information when you collect, review and export Amazon product reviews. Contact the developer at e@shiplang.com.

Review collection and export

When you start collection on a supported Amazon product or review page, the extension reads the reviews displayed there and requests additional review pages from that Amazon site. When a matching Woot review page is available, the extension can request review data from Woot. These requests may use your existing browser session with the relevant site. The extension does not read or store your Amazon or Woot password.

Processed review information can include reviewer names and profile links, review identifiers and links, review titles and text, ratings, dates, product variants, verified-purchase labels, helpful-vote counts, image and video links, and the time of collection. This information is used to display a review table, remove duplicates and produce the CSV file you choose to download.

Review processing and CSV generation take place in your browser. Review content and the product URLs you collect are not uploaded to the developer or ShipLang by this version of the extension. The originating websites receive the requests needed to retrieve their review pages. Opening a review, profile, image or video link contacts its destination.

Account and membership

Sign-in and payment open a separate hosted service window at crx.shiplang.com. The extension sends the product identifier, extension identifier, installed version, browser interface language and, if already saved, the account user identifier and local membership expiration time. This allows the service to return account and membership information for the correct product. Ordinary connection information, such as an IP address and browser information, accompanies requests to online services.

The login service uses Google sign-in and receives the email address and profile information permitted by that sign-in flow. Google authentication is handled on Google pages through the service's callback at login.chromeextensions.workers.dev. The extension does not receive your Google password. See the Google Privacy Policy.

ShipLang stores the email address, avatar and membership expiration information needed for login and membership, with an account identifier to associate the result with your account. The extension saves the returned account and membership record in Chrome's local extension storage and checks the product identifier and expiration time before enabling membership access.

Payment information is entered in the hosted checkout offered by the service; this extension does not collect or store card numbers. The checkout identifies the payment provider for your transaction. If the selected checkout uses PayPro Global or Stripe, that provider's policy applies: PayPro Global Privacy Policy and Stripe Privacy Policy.

Permissions and local storage

  • Storage: saves account and membership information and a flag indicating whether the account-specific uninstall feedback link has been configured. Storage remains local to this Chrome profile; this extension does not use Chrome Sync storage.
  • Supported Amazon sites: host access allows the extension to add collection controls, read the selected product's reviews and request review pages from the supported regional Amazon sites.
  • Woot: host access allows the extension to detect a matching open Woot review page and retrieve its reviews when that source is used.

The extension opens its own review table and service windows using browser APIs. It does not request access to all websites or to your full browsing history. When an account is available, the extension configures an uninstall feedback URL at crx.shiplang.com containing the account identifier, extension version and interface language. If you uninstall, Chrome may open that feedback page.

Retention and deletion

The review table holds its collected reviews in the browser while that collection page remains open. Downloaded CSV files remain on your device until you delete them. Local account information remains until you clear this extension's stored data or remove the extension.

ShipLang retains its login and membership data until you request deletion and does not retain backups of that data. To request account-data deletion, email support@shiplang.com, provide the extension name or its download link, and describe your request and reason. Requests are processed within seven days. Contact e@shiplang.com for questions about the extension's local data or this policy. Google and any payment provider apply their own retention and deletion policies to information handled by their services.

Other disclosures

The extension does not contain advertising or analytics tracking code and does not sell review data or account data. Only information needed for the collection, export, sign-in, membership and feedback functions described above is handled by this version.

The developer works with ShipLang as a technical service partner. This product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and sign-in and payment technology that developers use to build products. This extension is independent of Amazon and Woot and is not their official product.
