Privacy Policy

Extension: Amazon Review Exporter - CSV & Bulk Reviews
Effective date: November 13, 2026

Purpose and review data

This independent extension collects reviews available on supported Amazon pages and matching Woot review pages, displays them for selection, removes duplicates and exports CSV files. Review data may include public reviewer names, text, ratings, dates, public profile or review links, images and videos. The working review list is held in extension page memory. Downloaded CSV files remain on your device until you delete them. Website restrictions and unavailable fields may limit the result.

Local storage and permissions

The storage permission saves account and membership information and a flag recording whether the uninstall feedback link has been configured. Website host access allows review-page collection and requests to the Amazon country sites, www.woot.com and electronics.woot.com listed in the extension manifest. The extension uses these permissions for review collection, opening its results page, membership verification and export reporting. It does not request access to all websites.

Network requests and export reporting

Review requests go to the relevant Amazon or Woot website and may use your existing site session. After a CSV download is initiated, the extension sends the normalized Amazon product URL (domain and ASIN, without tracking or search parameters) to https://shiplang.com/extensions/post/api_amazon_domains for export reporting. The review text and CSV file are not sent by this reporting request. The browser may include standard network metadata such as your IP address in requests. We do not specify a retention period for product export reports because no deletion schedule has been verified; contact the developer for retention or deletion questions.

Optional sign-in and payments

When you choose the payment action in the membership prompt, the extension opens https://crx.shiplang.com. This flow sends the product AppId, extension ID, version, interface language and, if available, user ID and locally saved membership expiry. ShipLang.com stores the email address, avatar and membership expiry needed for account and membership services and protects this data. According to the service policy, account data is kept until a deletion request is received, without backup retention. To request deletion, email support@shiplang.com with the extension name or download link and the requested action and reason; requests are handled within seven days.

Google sign-in may be used. Payment provider availability depends on the checkout shown to you; the service may use PayPro Global or Stripe. Review the provider policy before proceeding: Google Privacy Policy, PayPro Global Privacy Policy, Stripe Privacy Policy.

Uninstall feedback

After an account identifier is available for this product, the extension configures an uninstall feedback URL once. On uninstall, Chrome may open a page at crx.shiplang.com containing the user ID, language and extension version.

Control and deletion

Clear extension data or uninstall the extension to remove its local account state. Close the results tab to discard its in-memory review list. Delete downloaded CSV files separately. Server-side account deletion requires contacting support@shiplang.com; export-reporting questions can be sent to the developer.
