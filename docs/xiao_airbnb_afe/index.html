Privacy Policy — Airbnb Photo Downloader

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy describes the extension Airbnb Photo Downloader (product ID: xiao_airbnb_afe). Contact the developer at e@shiplang.com.

Photo downloads

The extension reads the currently opened Airbnb listing and accessible photo metadata to identify photo candidates. It requests selected images directly from Airbnb's muscache.com image servers to validate and save their original bytes, either as individual files or in a locally generated ZIP. Photo URLs, listing IDs, selections and task status remain in your browser's local extension storage. Image bytes and ZIP data are held temporarily in memory and are released when the task finishes or is cancelled. Photos are not uploaded to the developer or ShipLang. Airbnb and its image servers receive normal network request information, such as your IP address, when an image is requested. Image requests omit account cookies.

Account and payments

Sign-in and membership payments open hosted pages on crx.shiplang.com. The extension sends the product ID, extension ID, interface language, extension version, and any previously stored ShipLang account UID and membership expiry to that service. Account and membership results are stored locally and used to determine access. ShipLang.com stores the email address, avatar and membership expiration required for login and membership, and protects this data. Payment details are entered on the hosted payment provider's pages, not collected by this extension. Provider-specific terms apply to any provider actually offered at checkout. A payment page, rather than this extension, supplies current prices.

Where Google sign-in is used, see Google's privacy policy. Where Stripe is offered, see Stripe's privacy policy; where PayPro is offered, see PayPro's privacy policy. The developer collaborates with ShipLang; this product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang supplies AI development software and login and payment infrastructure.

Trial and local storage

Single-photo downloads are free. Batch and ZIP tasks share five locally stored trials. One trial is consumed only if all items finish successfully; failures and cancellations do not count. Valid membership does not consume trials. The Simplified Chinese interface is free. Trial balance is not reset by closing the popup or updating the extension. Local storage also retains the latest task, selected photo IDs, account result, and whether the uninstall feedback URL has been configured. Local data is not synced by this extension.

Uninstall feedback

For a signed-in account, the extension configures a ShipLang uninstall feedback URL containing the account UID, interface language and extension version. Chrome may open this URL when the extension is removed. You can remove the extension and its local account data through Chrome extension management.

Permissions

  • storage: retain trial balance, account status, selections and task progress locally.
  • downloads: save selected photos or ZIP files, inspect this extension's tracked download IDs, cancel its active task, and reveal saved files.
  • offscreen: validate images and build local Blob and ZIP resources outside the popup.
  • alarms: periodically reconcile pending tasks after the background worker sleeps.
  • Airbnb domain access: read photos from supported regional Airbnb listing pages, and inspect the active listing URL on those domains.
  • muscache.com access: request the selected photo resources.

No analytics or advertising SDK is included. No remote executable scripts are loaded. There is no clipboard, browsing history, password, camera, microphone, or all-sites permission.

Retention and deletion

Local account data can be removed by clearing this extension’s storage or uninstalling the extension. Trial balance and the latest task remain until extension storage is cleared or the extension is uninstalled. Downloaded files remain in your chosen download folder until you delete them. ShipLang account data is retained until a deletion request and is not backed up, according to the service information supplied for this integration. To request service-side deletion, email support@shiplang.com with the extension name or store link and your request and reason; the service's stated processing period is seven days. Payment providers may retain transaction information under their own policies and obligations.

Changes and contact

Changes to processing will be reflected in an updated policy date. For extension-related privacy questions, contact e@shiplang.com.
