Bitcoin Price Monitor

Privacy Policy · Updated 2026-11-14

This policy describes the Chrome extension Bitcoin Price Monitor, developed independently by the developer reachable at e@shiplang.com.

Market prices and local storage

The extension displays Bitcoin prices and an optional list of cryptocurrency prices in US dollars. Public quote requests go to CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com), with Coinbase Exchange (api.exchange.coinbase.com) used as a Bitcoin fallback. These providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and the requested public market data. The extension does not attach account details, wallet addresses, browsing history or search text to price requests.

Quotes, source timestamps, cache state and refresh scheduling information are stored locally in your Chrome profile. Searches filter the downloaded list on your device. Market data may remain until it is replaced or you remove the extension or clear its local data. We do not operate a market-data collection server or use browser sync for this data.

Permissions

The storage permission stores local caches and optional legacy account information. The alarms permission refreshes prices periodically while the popup is closed. Host access is restricted to the CoinGecko API and the Coinbase BTC-USD endpoint. No content scripts read the websites you visit. The extension does not access your browsing history, wallet, private keys, contacts or portfolio.

Optional legacy account connection

Basic price viewing does not require an account or payment. The extension retains an account connection receiver for compatibility with earlier versions. Only when an account connection URL is explicitly opened does it open the external ShipLang account portal at crx.shiplang.com. That request includes the product identifier, installed extension identifier, version and interface language, and any existing account identifier and local membership expiry. A matching response from the account service is stored locally as account information.

For users with an existing stored account identifier, an uninstall feedback URL may send that identifier, extension version and interface language to crx.shiplang.com when the extension is removed. Users without a stored account identifier do not use that uninstall route.

ShipLang.com stores the email address, avatar and membership expiration needed for account and membership services and protects those records. Records are kept until a deletion request is received, without retaining backups. Request deletion at support@shiplang.com, identifying the extension name or download link and explaining your request and reason. Requests are processed within seven days. If Google sign-in is used, Google's own privacy policy applies to that sign-in. Payment details entered on an optional external account or payment page are governed by that service and its payment provider; they are not collected by the extension's price interface.

The developer and ShipLang are collaborators. This independent product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and account and payment technology used by developers to build products.

No advertising or tracking

The extension does not include analytics, advertising trackers or remote executable code. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising, credit decisions or profiling. Displayed public market prices are not records of your financial transactions.
