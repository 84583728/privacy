Privacy Policy

Download Desk — Find Recent Downloads
Effective date: November 6, 2026

Download Desk opens a downloads workspace in a new browser tab. It provides recent downloads, local search and filters, and controls supported by Chrome. This policy describes the extension’s current features and data practices.

Download records stay in your browser

The basic shortcut to Chrome's Downloads page does not require access to download records. The workspace asks for the optional downloads permission only when you click the enable button. After permission is granted, the workspace reads Chrome's retained download records, including filenames, local paths, source addresses, file types, sizes, dates, and download states. These records are used to display and search your downloads, reveal a file in its folder, and pause or resume supported tasks. The extension does not scan your disk or read file contents. Incognito downloads are excluded from the workspace.

Download records, local paths, filenames, source addresses, and search queries are not sent to the developer, account service, advertisers, or analytics services. Search and filtering run locally. Download records remain in Chrome's own download history; the extension does not make a separate persistent copy of full download records. Query conditions and pagination cursors, including download record IDs and timestamp boundaries, are kept in browser session storage to continue lists without duplicates and avoid counting the same automatic refresh or query page more than once. These session values are cleared when the browser session ends.

Local settings and trial state

The storage permission saves your opening preferences, the start time of your enhanced-feature trial, remaining successful trial uses, account membership data, and whether an uninstall link has already been configured. These values are stored in this browser profile, not in browser sync. The trial starts on the first successful enhanced operation, and enhanced access continues until both at least 14 days have elapsed and 60 successful uses have been consumed. A trial use means one successful user-initiated enhanced search or filter. Ordinary history browsing, scrolling, subsequent pages, automatic refreshes, empty results, and failures do not consume trial uses. Under the current product rules, the Simplified Chinese browser language is free.

Optional account and payments

No account is required to use the basic features or the available trial. Only when you choose to sign in or view payment options does the extension open a separate HTTPS account or checkout window provided by ShipLang. The request includes the product AppId, extension ID, version, browser interface language, and, if already saved, your account identifier and locally saved membership expiry. Download records and search terms are not included.

ShipLang.com stores the account email, avatar if provided, account identifier, and membership expiry needed for login and membership. The extension stores only the account identifier, email, product identifier, and membership expiry returned to it. Account information is retained by ShipLang until you request deletion; its stated retention policy does not keep backups after deletion. The developer and ShipLang are independent collaborators. This product does not represent ShipLang; ShipLang supplies development and account/payment infrastructure.

If you use Google sign-in, Google's Privacy Policy applies. The payment provider shown at checkout handles payment details; the extension does not receive card information. If the checkout uses PayPro Global or Stripe, consult the PayPro Global Privacy Policy or Stripe Privacy Policy, respectively, before completing payment. Current prices and providers are displayed by the checkout service.

Uninstall and deletion

For signed-in users, the extension sets a ShipLang uninstall feedback address once, containing the saved account identifier and extension version. Chrome may open that page when the extension is removed. Signing out removes the locally stored account record. Removing the extension clears its extension-local and session storage and revokes its download access; it does not delete your downloaded files or Chrome download history.

To request deletion of server account data, email support@shiplang.com, provide the extension name or download link, and describe your request and reason. ShipLang states that requests are processed within 7 days. For questions about this extension, contact the developer at the address below.

Data use and third parties

The extension contains no advertising, analytics, remote executable scripts, or content scripts that inspect websites. It does not sell personal data or use it for advertising. Data is used only to provide the disclosed extension features and account services. The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Third-party sign-in and payment pages have their own policies.

Changes

Material changes to data practices will be reflected in an updated policy and, where needed, an in-product disclosure before the changed practice is used.
