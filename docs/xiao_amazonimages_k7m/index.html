Privacy Policy

Amazon Product Image Downloader

Effective date: 2026-11-06 · Product ID: xiao_amazonimages_k7m

This policy explains how this browser extension handles information when you preview and download product-gallery images from supported Amazon product pages, use a free product allowance, or sign in and purchase membership.

1. Product-page information and images

When you open the extension on a supported product page, it reads the product identifier (ASIN), product title, gallery metadata and image addresses from that page. It does not scan your other tabs or collect a general browsing history. Product information is used to show previews, name downloaded files and identify whether a product has already used a free allowance.

Images are requested from Amazon image servers, including media-amazon.com and ssl-images-amazon.com. Those servers receive ordinary network-request information, such as your IP address and request headers, under their own practices. The extension omits account credentials when fetching images for export. Image files and ZIP archives are prepared locally in the browser. The extension's product-image features do not upload product content, downloaded images or your ASIN list to the developer or ShipLang.

2. Local storage and download information

The extension stores previously used free-product ASINs and membership information in local extension storage. Temporary reservations and this extension's download identifiers are kept in browser-session storage to avoid charging for failed or cancelled exports and to prevent simultaneous exports from exceeding the available allowance. It does not use browser sync storage.

The extension uses the browser download API to start downloads and check completion or interruption for download IDs it created. Its production code does not enumerate your entire download history. Completed files and download entries are also managed by Chrome and your operating system.

3. Sign-in, membership and payments

Sign-in and payment are opened only after you choose the relevant control. These services are provided through ShipLang. The service request includes the product ID, extension ID, extension version, interface language, and an existing user identifier and locally known membership expiry when available. The extension accepts membership results only from its designated service window, trusted service origins and matching product ID, and saves the returned account and membership information locally.

ShipLang stores the email address, avatar and membership expiry needed for login and membership, and protects that information. ShipLang's supplied retention policy keeps these service records until you request deletion and does not retain backup copies after deletion. You can request deletion at support@shiplang.com, providing the extension name or its download link and explaining your request and reason. Requests are processed within seven days of receipt.

Google sign-in is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Payment details are handled by the checkout provider shown to you, rather than by the extension. The extension does not ask for or store payment-card numbers. Where checkout uses Stripe or PayPro Global, their respective policies apply: Stripe Privacy Policy and PayPro Global Privacy Policy. The checkout determines which provider is actually used.

The developer and ShipLang are independent partners. This product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and the login and payment technology used by the developer.

4. Permissions and their purposes

  • Storage: retains free-product usage, account and membership information, temporary export reservations, and a flag recording whether an uninstall-feedback link has been configured.
  • Downloads: starts image or ZIP downloads and monitors this extension's own download IDs so that successful completion, cancellation and interruption can be handled correctly.
  • Supported Amazon marketplace hosts: reads product-gallery information and the address of the matching active product tab. The extension does not request access to every website.
  • Amazon image hosts: obtains image previews and export files from the two supported Amazon image-domain families.

The extension does not request the broad tabs permission, cookies access, Google identity APIs or network-interception permissions. Login happens in an external service window.

5. Uninstall feedback

After an account identifier is available, the extension configures a ShipLang uninstall-feedback link once. If Chrome opens this link when the extension is uninstalled, it contains the user identifier and extension version. This is separate from reading or uploading product pages.

6. Retention and deletion

Local free-product and account records remain until you remove the extension's local data or uninstall it. Temporary job records are cleared when work finishes or is cancelled and are not retained beyond browser-session storage. Removing the extension does not delete files you downloaded or automatically clear Chrome's download history; manage those through Chrome and your operating system.

Uninstalling the extension does not itself delete a server-side account, cancel a subscription or request deletion from a payment provider. Contact ShipLang for service-account deletion and use the provider's account or checkout support for billing and provider-held records. You may also contact the developer for help locating the appropriate service.

7. Analytics, advertising and sharing

This extension does not include behavioral analytics, advertising trackers or third-party statistics scripts. It does not sell product-page data or browsing history. Account and payment services process the information necessary for their functions under the policies linked above; this page does not describe them as having no independent processing.

ShipLang account-data requests: support@shiplang.com.
