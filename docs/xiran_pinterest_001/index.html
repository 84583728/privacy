Privacy Policy

Pinterest Bulk Image Downloader
Effective date: 2026-11-15

This extension lets you select images that have loaded on Pinterest pages and save them to your device as ZIP archives. This policy describes the extension itself. Pinterest and your browser operate under their own policies.

Information used for the feature

On supported Pinterest pages, the extension reads Pin image addresses, available image sizes, image descriptions, Pin links and the current page route. These are used to show thumbnails, merge size variants of the same media, remember your selection during the browser session and download selected files. It does not read Pinterest passwords or payment details.

Where information is stored

Image records, page routes, selections and the most recently completed task's failed items are stored in Chrome's extension session storage. They are not synced to another device or sent to a developer-operated server. Session data is cleared when the browser session ends or the extension is disabled, reloaded or removed. Image bytes are held temporarily in memory for packaging. ZIP files are saved using Chrome's download system and remain on your device until you delete them.

Network requests and other services

To download an image, the extension requests its HTTPS address from i.pinimg.com, Pinterest's image delivery service. It tries the original image address first and may fall back to the image size exposed by the page. Image requests omit cookies and other browser credentials. Like other network services, the image host can receive ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser headers. See Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

The extension has no analytics, advertising, login, membership or payment service. It does not send image collections, selections, browsing history or downloaded files to us. We do not sell personal information.

Permissions

  • downloads: save ZIP files and check the completion or interruption of downloads initiated by the extension. It does not use this permission to inspect unrelated download records.
  • storage: keep the current browser session's image collection, selection and failed items locally. On installation or upgrade, known legacy account-storage fields and a legacy uninstall redirect are removed; legacy account information is not transmitted.
  • i.pinimg.com access: fetch selected images from Pinterest's image host.
  • Pinterest page access: a content script runs only on the Pinterest domains declared in the extension manifest, including supported regional sites, to discover Pin images. It is not injected into unrelated websites.

Links, support and your choices

The example link opens Pinterest. The support link opens Gmail with only our support address prefilled; no images or page information are attached automatically. If you choose to contact us, your email provider handles that message and we use the information you voluntarily provide to answer your request. See Google's Privacy Policy for Gmail.

To clear the extension's session data, close the browser or disable and re-enable the extension. You can also remove it through Chrome's Extensions page. To remove saved images or archives, delete the downloaded files from your device. The extension cannot delete copies you have made elsewhere.

Limited use

The extension handles page information only to provide its visible image collection, selection, packaging and download features. Our use of this information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. Information is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or unrelated purposes.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion of information you voluntarily sent to support, contact e@shiplang.com. We will handle such requests within seven days where applicable. We may update this policy when the feature or its data handling changes; the effective date will be updated.

This is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.
