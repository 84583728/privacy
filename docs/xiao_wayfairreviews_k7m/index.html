Privacy Policy

Wayfair Reviews Exporter - CSV is an independently developed browser extension for collecting available product reviews from supported Wayfair pages and preparing them for local viewing and CSV export. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Wayfair.

What the review feature processes

When you start an export, the extension reads the current supported product page. It may process the product title and URL, review counts, reviewer display names, publicly displayed locations, ratings, review text, buyer-verification labels, dates, and review-image URLs. It uses the page's controls to request additional reviews from Wayfair and compares the collected count with the count available on the page.

The latest result, a small recent-result cache, and task progress are stored in the extension's local browser storage. The cache retains up to five recent export results within the extension's storage budget; older cached results may be removed as new results are saved. Review records are not sent by the extension to the developer or the account service. CSV files are saved to the location chosen by your browser, and copying a CSV places it on your device's clipboard at your request.

Showing review-image previews requests the images from their original hosting locations. Those image hosts receive the usual network request information, such as your IP address, under their own policies. Wayfair also receives requests made by the product page when more reviews are loaded. The extension does not bypass the site's access controls.

Local usage and account data

The extension keeps product identifiers for its local allowance and may retain active task state to prevent duplicate operations. These records remain in this browser profile and are not stored with browser sync. Account and membership responses are stored locally as product-specific user information so the extension can determine your access.

When you choose to sign in or open payment, the extension opens a hosted HTTPS page provided by ShipLang.com. The request includes the product AppId, extension ID, extension version, browser interface language, and, when already available, your account user identifier and locally saved membership expiry time. The account service returns account and membership information for the current product.

ShipLang.com stores the email address, avatar, and membership expiry time needed to provide sign-in and membership services and protects this information. It retains this service data until you request deletion and does not retain backups after deletion. To request deletion, contact support@shiplang.com, include the extension name or download link, and explain your request and reason. Requests are handled within seven days.

The developer works with ShipLang as a technology partner. This extension does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and the sign-in and payment technology used by the developer.

External sign-in and payment providers

Google sign-in may use the hosted callback at https://login.chromeextensions.workers.dev/oauth2callback to return the authentication response to the account service. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes information under the Google Privacy Policy. Payments take place on the hosted service and the payment provider offered there. If PayPro Global is offered, its Privacy Policy applies. If Stripe is offered, its Privacy Policy applies. The extension's JavaScript does not read or store payment-card numbers.

Permissions and network scope

Local storage: saves export results, task state, usage records, and account and membership information.

Supported page access: the packaged content script runs on HTTPS pages under wayfair.com, wayfair.ca, wayfair.co.uk, and wayfair.de, so it can read review information and activate the site's review-loading controls when requested.

The extension does not request access to your browsing history, cookies, or all websites. It does process the current supported product URL as the source of an export. It does not continuously record the pages you visit across the web. All executable extension code is packaged locally; account and checkout pages run as external web pages.

Uninstall feedback

After an account identifier becomes available, the extension may configure an uninstall-feedback page. If your browser opens that page after removal, its URL includes your user identifier, the extension version, and the interface language so the service can associate feedback with the product and account. This does not include your exported review records.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can remove the extension to delete its local extension storage, or clear that storage through your browser's extension tools. Downloaded CSV files and copied clipboard content are separate from extension storage; delete those through your device's file and clipboard controls. Removing the extension does not cancel or delete a hosted service account or any payment-provider record.

For account-service deletion, use the service contact described above. External providers handle their own records under their policies and applicable obligations. Only export content you are entitled to access and use, and take care when sharing files that contain other people's public comments or display names.

Use and sharing

The extension uses the data described here to perform the requested review export and support user-initiated account and membership operations. It does not include advertising or analytics trackers and does not sell review records. It does not use review data for personalized advertising or credit decisions.
