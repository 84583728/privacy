Privacy Policy

YouTube™ Automatic HD/FPS/4k/8k High Quality

Effective date: 2026-11-07 · Product identifier: xiao_youtubehd_a7k

This policy describes data handled by this browser extension and the account services it opens. The extension automatically applies your video quality preferences on www.youtube.com. It is independently developed and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

Video quality and local settings

The extension reads the current YouTube player's available quality levels, playback state and current video identifier to apply your saved preferences. These values are processed in the page and in the extension. To count the free trial once per successful video playback period, the extension also stores the current trial video identifier, an opaque session token, browser tab and frame identifiers, a pending or completed flag, and the remaining trial balance locally. Pending reservations expire after two minutes or are released when the operation fails, is cancelled, or the page closes. The extension does not send video titles, video identifiers, page URLs, watch history, video content, or trial events to the developer or the account service.

Chrome local extension storage holds the enable switch, selected quality, apply-once preference, higher-quality and fallback preferences, high-frame-rate preference, and console logging preference. It also holds the local free-trial balance and session records described above, account and membership information returned by the account service, and a flag indicating whether the uninstall feedback address has been configured. The extension does not use Chrome sync storage.

Optional sign-in and membership

When you choose sign-in or membership, the extension opens a page on crx.shiplang.com. The address includes the interface language, product identifier, browser extension identifier, extension version, and, when available, your account user identifier and locally saved membership expiry. The returned account object is stored locally for this product. It may contain your account identifier, email address, avatar and membership expiry. Account information is not copied into the YouTube page.

ShipLang.com provides the sign-in and membership service and protects the email, avatar and membership-expiry data needed to provide that service. The developer cooperates with ShipLang; this product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang supplies AI development software and sign-in and payment technology for developers building products with AI.

If you use Google sign-in, Google's handling of your account is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Checkout takes place on external payment pages. Depending on the provider displayed during checkout, the PayPro Global Privacy Policy or Stripe Privacy Policy applies. This extension does not collect or store card numbers or bank credentials.

Uninstall feedback

After an account user identifier has been returned for this product, the extension configures a one-time Chrome uninstall feedback address on crx.shiplang.com. If Chrome opens that address when you uninstall, it includes the interface language, account user identifier and extension version. The address is configured once; uninstalling removes local extension storage.

Permissions

  • storage: saves the local preferences, account state and trial records described above.
  • scripting: registers the local player-control script in the page world and a separate script that transfers quality preferences. It is limited to the authorized YouTube host.
  • www.youtube.com host access: applies quality and frame-rate preferences to YouTube web players and displays the current quality on the extension badge. It does not authorize access to other websites.

The extension's executable scripts are included in the installed package. It contains no analytics or advertising SDK, and does not download or execute remote program code. External account and payment pages remain separate websites and process ordinary connection data, such as IP addresses, under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences, trial balance and account state remain in your browser until the extension is removed or its local data is cleared. Completed trial session records are removed when their tab closes or are replaced when another video uses the same document context. Pending reservations expire after two minutes. Browser restarts and extension updates do not reset an exhausted trial. Restoring quality defaults only resets quality preferences and does not erase account or trial records. ShipLang account data is retained until you request deletion; deleted service data is not kept in backups.

To request deletion of ShipLang account data, email support@shiplang.com, identify the extension by name or download link, and describe your request and reason. Requests are processed within 7 days after receipt. Removing the extension does not itself request deletion of service-side account data. Payment providers may retain transaction information under their own policies.

Contact and changes

For extension support, privacy questions, or developer contact, email e@shiplang.com. Material changes to these practices will be reflected in an updated effective date on this page.
