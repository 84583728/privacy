Calculator with History
Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026 · Product ID: xiao_historycalc_k7m

Calculator with History is a browser extension for scientific calculations, reusable calculation history, unit conversion and discount calculations. Localized store names may describe these features differently. This policy covers version 2.2 and later versions with the same data practices. Developer contact: e@shiplang.com.

Calculations stay on your device

Expressions, answers, memory values, up to 200 history entries, favorites, unit conversion inputs, discount inputs and display preferences are stored in the extension's local browser storage. They are not uploaded to us or to a calculation service, and are not synchronized through browser sync. Arithmetic and conversions work offline. There are no analytics, advertising scripts or automatic collection of browsing history.

If you deliberately choose the context-menu calculation command, Chrome supplies only the text you selected. We open that selection as an expression in a local extension window. The extension does not inject scripts into websites, scrape pages or read the contents of other tabs. Unsupported text is rejected as a mathematical expression.

Permissions and user actions

  • storage saves your calculator state and, if you choose to sign in for voluntary support, the limited account information described below.
  • contextMenus adds a command to calculate selected text and a command to open a separate calculator window.

The extension requests no website host permissions and no permission to read tabs, browsing history or screen information. Copying happens only when you click a copy control. CSV export creates a local file only when you request it. Links to examples open local extension pages; support and privacy links open external websites when clicked.

Optional support and payment service

All calculator features are available without signing in or paying. Clicking the optional support button opens a separate ShipLang login/payment page at crx.shiplang.com. If you are not signed in, that page may ask you to sign in first. The extension includes the product ID, extension ID, extension version, browser interface language and, when already available, your account user ID and locally stored membership expiry in this request. It does not include your expressions or calculation history.

Following a validated service callback, the extension may store your user ID, product ID, account name, email address, avatar URL and membership expiry locally. Account data is accepted only from the service window at https://crx.shiplang.com for this product. Your calculator functions do not depend on membership status. The extension does not receive or store card numbers, bank credentials or payment security codes.

ShipLang.com states that it retains the email, avatar and membership expiry information needed for its login and membership services, protects that information, retains it until a deletion request and keeps no backup after deletion. Its account identifier is used to associate requests with your account. To request service-account deletion, email support@shiplang.com with the extension name or download link and describe your request and reason. ShipLang states requests are processed within seven days. Payment providers may have separate legal retention obligations, as described in their own policies.

After an account user ID is available, the extension registers a one-time uninstall link. If you uninstall after registration, Chrome may open a ShipLang uninstall page with the user ID, extension version and interface language. This does not send calculation data.

Third-party services

Google sign-in, when selected on the support page, is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Payment options displayed by the external service may be provided by PayPro Global or Stripe; the relevant provider's policy applies to information you enter there. Opening third-party pages also exposes normal network information, such as your IP address, to the page operator. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads no external scripts, fonts or tracking resources.

The developer and ShipLang cooperate independently. This product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and login/payment infrastructure that developers can use to build products.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until removed, replaced by newer records at the 200-entry limit, or deleted when the extension is uninstalled. Starred records are protected from automatic cleanup. Use “Clear unstarred” to remove ordinary history, or delete individual entries including favorites. Uninstalling removes extension-local settings, inputs and account data; downloaded CSV files remain where you saved them and can be deleted manually. Clearing local data does not delete a service-side account: use the contact above for that request.
