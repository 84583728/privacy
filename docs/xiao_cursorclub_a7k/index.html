Privacy policy

Custom Cursor for Chrome – Anime, Games & Characters

Effective date: 2026-11-07

This policy explains how this Chrome extension handles information when you select cursor themes, load theme images, or use its optional account and membership services. The extension changes cursor appearance on supported webpages. It does not need to read or upload webpage text, form entries, or browsing history to provide this feature.

Information kept on your device

The extension uses Chrome local extension storage to remember your selected theme, whether cursors are enabled, and the cursor mode. It saves the selected theme's identifiers and image addresses, and caches the three images for the currently selected theme as image data so that the selected cursor can be applied without downloading images from each webpage. Choosing another theme replaces the current image cache; the extension does not keep a complete downloaded library.

If you use the account service, the extension stores the service's account and membership response in a local userInfo record. This may include your internal account identifier, product identifier, email address, avatar and membership expiry time. It also stores a local flag indicating whether an uninstall feedback address has been registered. These settings use local storage, not Chrome account sync.

Cloud images

Theme previews and selected theme images are downloaded over HTTPS from img.bizbrz.com. Image requests identify the requested image and necessarily expose technical connection information, such as your IP address and browser user agent, to the image host. Selected-image downloads omit credentials and referrer information. Preview images suppress referrers; browser cookie and network controls still apply to image requests. It does not attach the address or content of the webpage you are visiting, your account identifier, or your membership information to image requests.

Webpage cursor styling uses the selected images cached locally. The extension does not make the webpages you visit download cursor images from the image host. Cloud images are static image files; extension code is bundled locally.

Accounts and membership

When you choose to open login or payment, a separate hosted page at crx.shiplang.com is opened. Its address includes the extension's product identifier, installed extension identifier, version, interface language, and, if available, your internal account identifier and locally stored membership expiry time. These values identify the product and account and allow the service to synchronize membership. The extension does not send your current webpage address or content to this service.

The hosted login service supports Google sign-in. Google handles its sign-in process under the Google Privacy Policy. The service uses login.chromeextensions.workers.dev as its Google sign-in callback, where the authorized profile and email response can be received. The account service receives the information needed to identify your account and returns account and membership information to the extension.

The payment provider is identified on the hosted checkout page. If that page uses Stripe, its processing is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy. If it uses PayPro Global, its processing is covered by the PayPro Global Privacy Policy. Payment details entered on a provider's page are handled by that provider. The extension does not contain a card-entry form or store payment card details.

After an account identifier is available, the extension registers a one-time uninstall feedback URL with Chrome. If you later uninstall the extension, Chrome may open that page at crx.shiplang.com with your internal account identifier, extension version and interface language. The extension does not send a record of the websites you visited.

Permissions

  • Storage: saves theme preferences, the selected image cache, account and membership information, and the uninstall-registration flag locally.
  • Access to img.bizbrz.com: downloads the theme images used for previews and the selected cursor cache.
  • Content scripts on HTTP and HTTPS webpages, including frames: apply the selected cursor style to normal webpage content and clickable elements. This access is used for cursor appearance. Browser-protected pages cannot be changed.

The extension does not request history, clipboard, downloads, or browser-account synchronization permissions. It does not include advertising or analytics code.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences, selected images and account information remain in the browser's extension storage until they are replaced, you clear the extension's data, or you uninstall the extension. Restoring the default cursor clears the selected theme and its image cache, but does not delete your hosted account. Removing local information does not delete server-side account records.

ShipLang.com states that it stores the email address, avatar and membership expiry information required for login and membership, together with the identifiers needed to associate the account with this product. It protects this information and retains it until you request deletion, without retaining a backup after deletion. To request deletion, email support@shiplang.com, provide the extension name or download link, and describe your request and reason. The service states that it processes these requests within seven days.

Image hosting, login and checkout providers receive technical connection information when you contact their services and may retain operational records under their own policies. Payment providers may retain transaction records under their policies and applicable requirements. The extension does not control those providers' independent records. Contact the developer if you need help identifying the service involved in a request.

Sharing and service relationship

Information is used to provide the cursor, image delivery, account and membership functions described here and is not sold or used by the extension for advertising or cross-site profiling. Information you choose to send in a support email is used to answer your request.

The developer and ShipLang are cooperating parties. This product does not represent ShipLang. ShipLang provides AI development software and login and payment infrastructure that developers can use to build products. The privacy practices of external login and payment providers are described in their linked policies.
